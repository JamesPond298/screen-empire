# Screen Empire project checklist

## Implemented — Stage 1 foundation

- Studio naming and beginner opening explanation.
- One shared **Next Week** clock; pages and reloads never advance time.
- Starting cash, weekly overhead, committed-cost view, and categorized transaction history.
- Studio-owned original movies and short TV seasons in comedy, drama, and action.
- Story concepts; small, medium, and large budgets; writer, director, and lead selection.
- Talent ability, genre fit, popularity, reliability, and hiring cost.
- Development, filming, post-production, and release stages using one rented production team.
- Deterministic critic/audience results and multi-week movie/TV revenue.
- Separate theatrical gross and the studio's fictional 45% share.
- Persistent catalog with direct project profit/loss (shared overhead excluded).
- Browser autosave, versioned import/export, pre-import backup, and confirmed reset.
- Responsive desktop/mobile interface and a short beginner tutorial.
- First-year survival milestone followed by continued sandbox play.
- Automatic local movie/TV title generator with distinct comedy, drama, and action styles.
- Saved draft suggestions, **Generate Another Title**, and protection for manually typed titles.
- Duplicate avoidance plus future-ready season/sequel title continuity helpers.

## Tested

- Automated checks: save migration defaults, stage boundaries, deterministic outcomes, and data integrity.
- Full local workflow: studio creation, affordable small production, weekly progression, release, revenue, catalog, and reload persistence.
- Finance checks: overhead once per week, staged production costs once, and revenue shares once.
- Import validation and protection against double-clicking the shared clock.
- First-year milestone logic is implemented; its full 52-week UI path still needs manual verification.
- Title-generator checks: all six genre/format banks, automatic and empty-field naming, manual-title confirmation, unchanged project settings, saved drafts, older saves, and title consistency in news/finances.

## Known limitations

- Stage 1 uses one production team and rented facilities only.
- Paid client work is intentionally reserved for Stage 2.
- Streaming licensing, negotiations, buyer market, and expanded News filters are reserved for Stage 3 onward.
- Browser saves remain on one browser/device unless exported.
- Final visual review in Edge or Chrome remains a manual check because the protected preview could not open a local-file URL.
